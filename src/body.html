Scan documents into clean, searchable PDFs

    Why scan with {{APP_NAME}}

    • Passport and ID copies. For a visa, a rental or a school application. Most scanner apps send your pages to their servers; {{APP_NAME}} can't, so a copy of your passport is only ever on your phone.
    • Money and work. Bank letters, tax forms, payslips and contracts are read and turned into a PDF right here, without an account.
    • Health. Prescriptions, test results and insurance claims stay between you and whoever you choose to send them to.
    • Receipts and expense claims. The PDF holds the receipt and nothing else: no phone model, no location, no date and time of the photo.
    • Documents for strangers. Landlords, employers, agencies and online forms get the pages you meant to send, not details about you or your phone.

    Good to know

    • What's on the page stays on the page. Names, numbers, signatures and photos on the document are in the scan. Cover anything you don't want to send before you take the photo.
    • Text recognition isn't perfect. Clear, flat, well-lit print reads best. Handwriting isn't read. Check important text before relying on it.
    • Your phone's own copies. Photos you import stay in your gallery as they were. {{APP_NAME}} never changes them.
